Which classes can I enquire about?+

Anand Bal Vidya Mandir offers classes from Nursery through Class 8. Please contact the school for current availability and admission details.

How do I apply for admission?+

Send an admissions enquiry using the form on this page. The school team can share the current process, required documents and next steps.

Where can I find the fee structure?+

Fee details are not published here. Please contact the school office to request the current fee information for the class you are interested in.

How can I ask about Robotics, AI & Coding Training?+

Use the contact form and select a class of interest, then mention robotics in your message. The programme is sponsored by Violet Spark Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

How do I access report cards or pay fees online?+

Secure student portals and online payments are not configured on this website. Please contact the school office for assistance and never send payment credentials by message.
